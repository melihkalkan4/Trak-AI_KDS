SPATIOTEMPORAL CV SCENARIO (#11)
================================
Implemented in src/cp25 _block_groups + LeaveOneGroupOut over block_id =
  year_block(5, linspace over year range) * n_space_clusters + KMeans(lat/lon, k=5).

SCENARIO = A  (cell holdout) = 'spatiotemporal block interpolation'.
Holding out one (year-block x space-cluster) cell leaves, in training, both other years of
the same regions and other regions of the same years. This is therefore NOT strict
spatiotemporal extrapolation (Scenario B, where the test year's ALL regions and the test
region's ALL years would be removed). The paper should name this regime 'spatiotemporal
block interpolation' (Scenario A), distinct from LOYO (temporal) and LOILO (spatial).

Scenario B (strict extrapolation) is an optional stricter variant; not run here to avoid
changing the published metric definition — flagged in HANDOFF as available on request.
