To the Editors,
**Data in Brief** (Elsevier)

**Re: Submission of a data article — "A cross-validation-ready district-year dataset of satellite, climate, soil and in-situ features for winter-wheat and sunflower yield modelling in Trakya, Türkiye"**

Dear Editors,

We are pleased to submit the enclosed data article for consideration in *Data in Brief*. The manuscript describes a derived, analysis-ready dataset deposited on Mendeley Data (reserved DOI 10.17632/f6d29w5zjk.1, CC-BY-4.0) that documents winter-wheat and oilseed-sunflower yield predictors and validation artefacts for 29 districts of Trakya, Türkiye.

The dataset is, to our knowledge, distinctive in one respect that motivates its release as a standalone data article: it publishes **temporal (leave-one-year-out) and spatial (leave-one-district-out) cross-validation folds computed on identical observations**, together with the extracted district-level Sentinel-2, NASA POWER (MERRA-2) and SoilGrids features, the complete feature-selection log, per-sample out-of-fold predictions for every layer × crop × model, and in-situ records from a field-surveyed 0.62 ha parcel. A column-level data dictionary, integrity checksums, and a dependency-light regeneration script are included, so that every headline derived table can be reproduced without a Google Earth Engine account. We believe this supports reuse in benchmarking cross-validation practice, meta-analysis of validation design, and teaching of honest model evaluation.

We confirm that:

- the data article and the dataset are original, are not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors approve the submission;
- the dataset is deposited in an approved repository (Mendeley Data) under a CC-BY-4.0 licence, with third-party source terms (Copernicus, NASA POWER, ISRIC SoilGrids, ESA WorldCover, SRTM, TÜİK) attributed in the deposit and manuscript;
- the work involves no human subjects, animal experiments, or social-media data;
- the authors have no competing interests to declare.
